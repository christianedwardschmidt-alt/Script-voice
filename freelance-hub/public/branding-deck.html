08 — Voice & Tone
Sound like a
smart colleague.
Not enterprise software. Not a startup trying too hard. Direct, confident, and human — respecting that freelancers run real businesses.
→ Clear No jargon. One idea per sentence.
→ Direct Say the thing. Skip the preamble.
→ Human Warm but not folksy. Real, not robotic.
→ Empowering We believe in you. Never condescending.
Write this
"Your taxes are estimated at $7,200. Here's how to reduce that."
Not this
"Our AI-driven tax optimization module leverages advanced deduction algorithms."
Write this
"Invoice sent. You'll get notified when it's opened."
Not this
"Your invoice has been successfully dispatched via our secure delivery pipeline."
Write this
"3 tasks overdue. Start with your highest-value client."
Not this
"You currently have 3 tasks that have exceeded their estimated completion date."
